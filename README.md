# Bahrain Sales Channel Intelligence

## Main dashboard

The Product Type view is now a built-in tab in `index.html`. It uses the active filters and shows activation counts, share and synthetic monthly revenue for Product A, B and C.

## Synthetic product scenario

- Product A: 67% of activations, BD 10 monthly revenue
- Product B: 23% of activations, BD 12 monthly revenue
- Product C: 10% of activations, BD 15 monthly revenue

Product assignments are synthetic because the source activation example contains no product field. They are not to be represented as verified sales data.

## Analytics assistant

The assistant is context-aware and provides comparisons based on the active filtered records. It does not send data externally.
